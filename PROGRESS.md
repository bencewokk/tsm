## Step 4: road hierarchy and pruning (done)
Code: `src/sim/hierarchy.ts` (new), strictness and spacing rules in `town.ts` (explore / densify), arterial route cost in `roads.ts`, class capacity in `sim.ts`, class rendering in `render/city.ts`, new road stats in `sim/metrics.ts`. `HIER.on = false` (or `?nohier=1` in the dev URL, `--nohier` in `npm run metrics`) switches back to the old unstructured growth for before / after.
- OLD CORE stays organic: within `coreR` of an original village / origin quarter nothing is straightened or pruned. Arterial edges inside a core become collectors (the historic main streets, 9.5 m cobbled; the other core lanes 6 m cobbled). A ring road (arterial) is laid on the old walls of the origin quarter where the land allows a full circle (not on the harbour town 4821, whose docks and water interrupt it).
- OUTSIDE the core: every new street lies on the district grid (regularity forced to 0.88, no 40-degree tolerance), a branch off an arterial must be a collector and at least 5 cells from the next junction on that arterial, locals only branch off collectors / locals, and a local that meets an arterial is promoted to a collector (so every arterial junction is arterial / collector). Every 6 months (and once at the start) a prune removes streets with no building, landmark or assembly on them that are short stubs (< 3.5 cells), diagonals (> 0.45 rad off the grid) or edges of triangle faces; the plots on them go too; a street is never removed if it would disconnect the network.
- Arterial routing follows valleys and passes (cost for height above the local low point), expensive straight climbs (the path runs along the contours and zigzags up: switchbacks emerge as contour zigzags, not as drawn hairpins) and a bigger penalty for steep ground.
- Class matters in the sim: the road raster holds the class (3 arterial / 2 collector / 1 local) and road capacity per zone weights a cell 2.4 / 1.5 / 1.0, which feeds congestion and car speed.
- Rendering: arterials 20 m, darker, planted median with trees (open stretches only); collectors 11.5 m; locals 7 m and lighter; old-town lanes cobbled.

## Round 5, task 2: cars switching colour (fixed)

## Round 5, task 3: road nodes not connected properly (fixed)
ROOT CAUSES. (1) Growth only snapped a new street to what it hit at its tip and nothing re-checked the graph afterwards, so a street could end a few metres short of another (a dead end beside a street, or two dead ends facing each other), a later street could cross an older one with no node, two growth passes could leave a 5-way node, and tiny stubs and narrow sliver wedges stayed. ### Step 6: trams as upgrades of successful bus corridors (done)
Code: `tramFit`, `rasterTrams`, `setLane` lane 3, `busSegTime` lane 3, the tram stop terms in `computeTransit` and `TUNE.tramFront` in `computeLandValue` (`src/sim/sim.ts`); `Traffic.route(..., wide)` (arterials and collectors only, from the nearest node on such a street); `TRAM_COST`, `TRAM_MONTH`, `TRAM_CAP`, `LANE_COST_KM[3]` (constants); `BusInfo.tram`, `modeTrips.tram` (protocol); the Lines panel's tram button and T names, the City panel's Tram trips row (`ui/hud.ts`); track bed, rails and long cars in `render/buses.ts`.
- A tram is the fourth lane step of a bus line: ordinary -> priority -> busway -> tram. Only a busway can take one, and only if its stops can be joined over arterials and collectors (street track needs a wide street) by a route no more than 1.5x the bus route plus 300 m; the Lines panel shows the tram button greyed with the reason otherwise ("no route on arterials or collectors between its stops", or the two lengths).

A tram has 200 seats (a busway bus 110, a bus 70) and runs at about the busway's speed (free flow on its own track with signal priority): it wins on seats, permanence and the frontage, not on speed.
- Frontage value effect: a tram stop gives most of a station's transit value to its walk catchment (0.75 of the station term against 0.35 for a bus stop), half a metro station's premium (fading out by 7 minutes' walk) and half of a station's access for the density field (mid-rise along the line, not towers); the street frontage of the track (cells within about 1.5 cells of it) gains 0.08 of land value.
- Trips: a journey that uses a tram and no metro line is a tram trip (counted apart; fares count it; the goals' metro share counts it with the car and the bus in the denominator).

### Step 7: AI arterials, bridges and bypasses as proposals (done)
Code: `src/sim/arterials.ts` (new: `RoadPlanner`, `ROADPLAN`), wired in `sim.ts` (monthly tick after the demand pressure, City hall decision through effect `road`, the cost of each choice in `choiceCost`, a transit street makes even an ordinary bus run as on a busway in `busSegTime`), `traffic.ts` (transit streets carry no cars), `pressure.ts` (`pTr`, transit trips per zone pair), protocol `roadPlan`, `render/buses.ts` (`setRoads`: transit streets as a terracotta surface, a waiting proposal as a dashed orange line with posts at its ends), the dev flag `?roadplan=0` (off) / `?roadplan=0.15` (lower threshold).
- The AI never builds a road. Twice a year (from month 24, at most one proposal a year, one open at a time) the highways department takes the strongest car missing-link candidates from the demand pressure system (zone pairs whose street route is 1.45x+ the straight line, still heavy this month) and searches for the missing link: from the node nearest each zone centre a shortest-distance tree, then every pair of arterial / collector nodes up to 700 m apart is a possible new street, and the eight that cut the pair's street distance most (distinct places) are laid out and scored. This finds the bridge over the river, the link past the yards, the bypass round the old core, and always joins the road hierarchy at both ends.
- arterialPressure, each term normalised to 0..1: 0.30 car demand (the pair's daily car trips / 3000) + 0.25 detour benefit (minutes saved x trips / 10000) + 0.15 land unlocked (vacant buildable cells beside it with no street within 2 cells / 40) + 0.10 freight / port access (an end in the port or heavy industry) - 0.20 viable transit alternative (2x the pair's transit share) - 0.20 demolition (buildings in the way / 15) - 0.35 historic core (old-core cells crossed / 6); a protected cell, more than 900 m of water or under 0.6 min saved rules a layout out. Threshold 0.25.
- The decision: "Build the arterial / road bridge / bypass" (the regional road fund pays half of $4M a km, $28M a km over water, $0.5M a building), "A transit street (over water: a bridge) for buses, trams, bikes and walkers only" (a collector closed to cars, 60% of the road and 70% of the bridge, all of the demolition; buses run at busway speed on it, a tram fits later, walks get shorter; a bridge gets a bus stop at each end), or "No new road" (nothing built, trust in the quarters at both ends -0.08; the department comes back after two years only if the pressure has grown by 0.05, titled "Again: ...").
